# LinkedIn — image gallery (F3)

## Facts about Oxide

You’re flipping through screenshots, icons, or exports in a folder — **F3** should show the picture, not a wall of binary.

**Oxide** does: **PNG**, **JPEG**, **GIF** open **in the terminal** with **F3**. Mark a few images (**Space**) and you get a **tabbed gallery** — flip with arrow keys or click the tab list. Still inside the file manager, no external viewer hop.

That’s rare in twin-panel tools. Oxide built it in.

Fair warning: it **really sings** on terminals that speak **Kitty**, **iTerm2**, or **Sixel** graphics; elsewhere you may get **half-block** fallback — usable, not always pretty. Right terminal, right protocol.
